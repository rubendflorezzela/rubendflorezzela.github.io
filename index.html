Human-Centered AI for Intelligent Transportation Systems

I am an Electronics Engineer and AI researcher working on Human-Centered AI for Intelligent Transportation Systems, with a focus on Intelligent Mobility. My interests include intelligent vehicles, computer vision, multimodal learning, trustworthy AI, and deployable intelligent systems.

My research explores AI systems that integrate human, environmental, and operational context to support safer and more adaptive mobility, with particular interest in multimodal perception, human-aware decision support, explainable AI, and real-world deployment.

I also have experience in university teaching and engineering laboratories, and I am interested in an academic trajectory combining research, teaching, advanced training, and international collaboration.

research lines

Four complementary directions within a broader research agenda in Human-Centered AI for Intelligent Transportation Systems, with a focus on Intelligent Mobility.

01

Multimodal Human & Scene Understanding

Integrating visual, behavioral, contextual, and temporal information to model both human state and the surrounding environment.

02

Human-Aware Assistance & Automation

Adaptive assistance, shared control, and decision support that account for human state, uncertainty, and driving context.

03

Trustworthy & Explainable AI

Robust, interpretable, and uncertainty-aware AI for safety-critical perception and decision-making.

04

Deployable & Edge AI

Efficient multimodal systems designed for real-time deployment under practical sensing, computational, and operational constraints.
