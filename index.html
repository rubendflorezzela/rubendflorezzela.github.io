About

Building interpretable multimodal AI systems for driver-state monitoring in intelligent transportation

I develop multimodal AI systems for real-time driver-state monitoring in intelligent transportation environments. My work combines computer vision, eye-tracking, behavioral analysis, and deep learning to model drowsiness, distraction, and cognitive workload under real-world conditions.

My research is grounded in human factors engineering and focuses on building interpretable, deployable systems that can support safer automated driving, driver assistance, and transportation safety evaluation through robust on-vehicle inference.

I am a researcher at the LIECAR Laboratory and lecturer at the Universidad Nacional de San Antonio Abad del Cusco (UNSAAC), Peru, recognized as a RENACYT Level V researcher by CONCYTEC. I serve as a peer reviewer for international scientific publications including Scientific Reports (Nature Portfolio) and ACM Transactions on Intelligent Systems and Technology.

Vision

Research Vision

My long-term goal is to develop interpretable multimodal systems that understand driver vulnerability in real-world transportation environments, bridging driver-state sensing, deployable AI, and human factors engineering. I aim to establish a research line where edge computing, explainability, and human-centered system design converge to build monitoring systems that are not only accurate, but trustworthy enough to inform automated driving, transportation safety, and system design guidelines.

Focus Areas

Research Interests

Driver State Modeling for Automated Driving

Real-time modeling of drowsiness, distraction, cognitive workload, and driver readiness in intelligent and automated driving contexts.

Human Factors AI for Transportation Safety

Investigating how multimodal sensing and AI can support human-centered system design, safety evaluation, and adaptive assistance.

Multimodal Perception and Fusion

Combining gaze behavior, facial dynamics, head pose, and visual cues for robust driver-state estimation beyond single-modality systems.

Embedded AI for Real-Time Monitoring

Deploying deep learning models on edge platforms such as NVIDIA Jetson Nano for low-latency, on-vehicle inference.

Interpretable AI for Safety-Critical Systems

Designing AI systems that are transparent, auditable, and trustworthy enough to support deployment in transportation and other high-stakes domains.
