Human-Centered AI · Intelligent Vehicles · Safety-Critical AI

Researching deployable and trustworthy AI for intelligent transportation

I develop human-centered and deployment-aware AI systems for intelligent vehicles, with emphasis on driver-state understanding, multimodal perception, and safety-critical decision support.

My current work combines computer vision, gaze and behavioral dynamics, robustness evaluation, explainable AI, uncertainty-aware learning, and edge deployment to study how driver-monitoring and assistance systems behave beyond aggregate accuracy. I am particularly interested in models that remain reliable under distribution shift, constrained hardware, and asymmetric safety costs.

Recent research spans deployment-aware benchmarking of vision models for driver monitoring, conformal risk control for graded longitudinal fallback in Level 2 automated driving, and real-time embedded drowsiness monitoring on NVIDIA Jetson platforms.

I previously served as a contract lecturer at UNSAAC and contribute as a peer reviewer for journals including Scientific Reports and ACM TIST. Review activity is documented through ORCID.

PhD direction: preparing for Fall 2027 doctoral applications in Human-Centered AI, Intelligent Transportation Systems, Human-AI Interaction, and Intelligent Vehicle Systems. Open to research collaborations and prospective supervisor conversations. Get in touch →
