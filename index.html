Ruben Dario Florez-Zela

I develop multimodal AI systems for real-time driver-state monitoring in intelligent transportation environments. My work integrates computer vision, eye-tracking, gaze dynamics, facial landmark modeling, and deep learning to detect drowsiness, distraction, and cognitive load under real-world operating conditions — with a strong emphasis on deployable, on-vehicle inference.

My research is grounded in human factors engineering and focuses on building interpretable systems that are trustworthy enough to support safer automated driving, driver assistance, and transportation safety evaluation. A central challenge I address is the gap between laboratory-based cognitive monitoring research and practical deployment on resource-constrained embedded platforms such as the NVIDIA Jetson Nano.

I am a researcher at the LIECAR Laboratory and lecturer at the Universidad Nacional de San Antonio Abad del Cusco (UNSAAC), Peru, recognized as a RENACYT Level V researcher by CONCYTEC. I serve as peer reviewer for Scientific Reports (Nature Portfolio) and ACM Transactions on Intelligent Systems and Technology, among 9 international journals (14+ reviews, verified via ORCID).

Research interests: Driver State Estimation · Multimodal AI · Embedded Deep Learning · Human Factors Engineering · Intelligent Transportation Systems · Explainable AI · Computer Vision · Gaze Modeling · Transportation Safety
