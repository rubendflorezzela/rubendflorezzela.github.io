Ruben D. Florez-Zela

Human-Centered AI for Safe and Intelligent Transportation

LIECAR Laboratory · UNSAAC · Cusco, Peru
RENACYT Level V Researcher

I develop human-centered AI systems for driver-state monitoring in intelligent transportation systems, focusing on detecting drowsiness, distraction, and cognitive load under real-world driving conditions.

My research integrates computer vision, gaze dynamics, and deep learning, with emphasis on deployable inference in embedded platforms such as NVIDIA Jetson Nano.

I am particularly interested in understanding how human cognitive states—such as attention, workload, and situational awareness—interact with AI systems in safety-critical environments.

Unlike purely dataset-driven approaches, my work emphasizes real-time, deployable AI systems validated under real-world driving conditions.

I am actively seeking PhD positions (Fall 2027) in Human Factors Engineering, Intelligent Transportation Systems, and Human-Centered AI.
