Ruben Dario Florez-Zela

LIECAR Laboratory · UNSAAC · Cusco, Peru

I develop multimodal AI systems for real-time driver-state monitoring in intelligent transportation environments. My work integrates computer vision, eye-tracking, gaze dynamics, and deep learning to detect drowsiness, distraction, and cognitive load under real-world conditions, with a strong emphasis on deployable on-vehicle inference on resource-constrained edge hardware.

My research is grounded in human factors engineering, focusing on interpretable, trustworthy architectures that bridge laboratory-based cognitive monitoring and practical deployment on platforms such as the NVIDIA Jetson Nano. I am recognized as a RENACYT Level V researcher by CONCYTEC and serve as peer reviewer for Scientific Reports (Nature Portfolio), ACM TIST, and 7 other international journals.
